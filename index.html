Lorcana Wants

Build a Cardmarket wants list for a Lorcana set. Upload your collection and it lists only the cards you still need.

How this works

  1. Pick a set. Names and rarities are fetched live, so new sets appear here as soon as they are published — nothing here needs updating when a set releases.
  2. Say how many copies of each rarity you want. The defaults build a playset-style collection and leave the rare chase cards out.
  3. Optionally upload your collection export. Cards you already own are subtracted.
  4. Copy the result into Cardmarket under Buying → My Wants → your list, paste it into the decklist field, and press Add.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your collection file is read in your browser and never leaves your machine.

One set at a time i Cardmarket matches wants by card name, and the same name can appear in more than one set — I'm Stuck! is in both Rise of the Floodborn and Fabled. Generating one set at a time guarantees a reprint is never confused with the printing you meant.
