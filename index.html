Lorcana Wants

Build a Cardmarket wants list for a Lorcana set. Upload your collection and it lists only the cards you still need.

How this works

  1. Pick a set. Names and rarities are fetched live, so new sets appear here as soon as they are published — nothing here needs updating when a set releases.
  2. Say how many copies of each rarity you want. Everything starts at 0, so nothing is requested until you ask for it — a common set completion is 1 common, 2 uncommon, 3 rare and 4 of each super rare and legendary.
  3. Optionally upload your collection export. Cards you already own are subtracted.
  4. Copy the result into Cardmarket under Buying → My Wants → your list, paste it into the decklist field, and press Add.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your collection file is read in your browser and never leaves your machine.

2. How many of each?

Every rarity starts at 0. Raise the ones you want.

Epic, Enchanted and Iconic cards are alternate printings of cards already in the set, so asking for them adds copies to an existing line rather than a new one. Cardmarket's paste format has no way to say which printing you want, so you would need to pick those out by hand.

3. Your collections (optional)

Add as many as you like — everything you add is combined into one collection. So if yours is split across several Dreamborn collections, or some are public and some are not, mix links and files freely.

The collection has to be public. Your browser fetches it from Dreamborn directly — nothing is sent to this site.

Use a file for any collection you keep private. You can pick several at once. It needs the columns Set Number, Card Number, Variant and Count:

    Split into separate lists

    A Cardmarket wants list holds a limited number of unique cards, and the Shopping Wizard works over one list at a time — so a whole set has to be spread across several lists to be shoppable. The limit counts unique cards, not copies: four copies of one card is one entry.
